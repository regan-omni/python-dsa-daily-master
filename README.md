# Python DSA daily mastery app

This is built with PWA technology; you can open the website, and it can be installed on your phone as an app.

<br>

## How to install on your phone (locally)

To install the PyAlgo Daily Progressive Web App (PWA) on your phone, add it directly to your home screen from your mobile browser.

### On Android (Google Chrome)

1. Open Chrome and navigate to https://regan-omni.github.io/python-dsa-daily-master/.
2. Tap the three-dot menu icon (⋮) in the top-right corner of the browser.
3. Select Add to Home screen or Install app from the menu.
4. Confirm by tapping Install or Add in the pop-up prompt. The app icon will appear on your home screen.

### On iPhone / iOS (Safari)

1. Open Safari (note: installing PWAs on iOS works most reliably through Safari rather than third-party browsers) and go to https://regan-omni.github.io/python-dsa-daily-master/.
2. Tap the Share icon (the square with an upward-pointing arrow) located on the bottom navigation bar.
3. Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
